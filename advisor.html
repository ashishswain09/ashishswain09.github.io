    RuleValueWhat it means for you
    Exit tax on Irish/EU funds & ETFs %Applies to gains and dividends on UCITS ETFs (e.g. VWCE, CSPX). No annual exemption and losses can't offset other gains. Cut from 41% to 38% from 1 Jan 2026.
    Deemed disposal yrsEvery 8 years you're taxed on ETF gains as if you sold, even if you didn't. Drags long-term compounding.
    Capital gains tax (shares, investment trusts) %Individual shares and UK/US investment trusts. Losses offset gains; no deemed disposal.
    CGT annual exemption€ First €1,270 of gains each year is tax-free. Worth harvesting every year.
    Dividends from sharesTaxed as income at your marginal rate + USC + PRSI. File a W-8BEN with your broker so US withholding is 15%, which you credit against Irish tax.
    Deposit interest (DIRT) %Tax on savings interest. An Post State Savings (Savings Certs/Bonds) are tax-free and State-guaranteed.
    Higher-rate band (single)€ Income above this is taxed at 40% income tax, so pension contributions above it save 40%.
    Pension relief earnings cap€ Tax relief limits by age: under 30 → 15%, 30–39 → 20%, 40–49 → 25%, 50–54 → 30%, 55–59 → 35%, 60+ → 40% of salary.
    Auto-enrolment (MyFutureFund)Started 1 Jan 2026 for employees aged 23–60 earning over €20,000 with no workplace pension: 1.5% from you, matched 1.5% by employer and 0.5% by the State, rising in steps to 6/6/2 by 2036.
